01Supporting the Team

This fundraiser supports Mississauga Lady Jays U13 Select. Choose the player you support when requesting a card. Their totals show confirmed ticket sales.

02How to Enter

Each card costs $20. Request a card, pay by e-transfer, then select six distinct numbers from 1–49 after the organizer confirms payment.

03The 8 Draws

Eight designated official Lotto 6/49 draws count, starting December 2, 2026. Use only six main numbers, excluding bonus numbers and other prize features.

04How Bingo Works

Matches accumulate. Each selected number counts once and remains matched across the pool. Numbers need not all appear in the same draw.

05Winning the Pool

After Draw 8, cards with 6/6 matches win. If none reaches 6/6, the highest cumulative match count wins. An early 6/6 does not end this pool.

06Ties

Split the winner’s pot equally among tied winning cards at the end of the pool.

07The 50/50 Pot

Half of collected paid entry fees goes to winners; half supports the team. For $1,000 collected, each share is $500. The displayed total pot is gross paid entry fees.

08Registration & Payment

Ticket requests, payment confirmation and number selection must be completed by 10:00 p.m. December 2, 2026, Toronto time. Use your card reference in the e-transfer message. An entry is complete only after payment confirmation and number selection. Check your details carefully.

09Numbers Cannot Be Changed

On this website, numbers lock immediately upon confirmation. They stay fixed throughout the pool.

10Official Results

The organizer enters verified official draw numbers. The website updates matches, rankings and totals automatically, refreshing every 30 seconds. Results are not imported automatically.

11Final Winner Verification

Before payout, the organizer checks registration, payment, selected numbers, official results, final matches and tied cards.

12Fundraiser Administration

Organizers may correct recording errors. This fundraiser is separate from the official Lotto 6/49 lottery.

13Winner Payment

The organizer contacts verified winners through their account email to arrange e-transfer payment.

14Social Media

Team social channels may promote the fundraiser and share draw updates. Ask winners for permission before posting names or photos. Private contact and payment details are not published.

15Age Verification

Participants must be at least 18. Organizers verify eligibility before confirming an entry; the website does not verify age automatically.
