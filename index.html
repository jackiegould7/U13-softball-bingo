Proposed format — not approved rules

  1. Supporters select six distinct numbers between 1 and 49.
  2. For each of eight scheduled Lotto 6/49 draws, an organizer records the six main drawn numbers.
  3. A number counts as a hit once if it appears in any included draw; the demo shows total unique hits for each card.
  4. All paid-entry, prize, tie-breaking and eligibility requirements must be established in approved rules before operating.
  5. Check charitable eligibility, lottery licensing, online sales requirements, permitted raffle mechanics, data protection and any approved electronic gaming system with the Ontario regulator.

This draft is inspired by the idea of the linked bingo fundraiser, but is newly written code and is not a copy of their system. It has no real-time database, payment service, public administrator login, or legally compliant drawing infrastructure.

Ontario regulator: Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario (AGCO).
